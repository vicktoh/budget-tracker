# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kano State health-sector MDA finance users, statewide Viewers, and administrators use the platform during recurring reporting and oversight work. MDA users record traceable funding and expenditure data for their assigned organizations; Viewers inspect entries, supporting evidence, warnings, and comments across MDAs; administrators manage reference data, reporting controls, publications, imports, exports, and system-wide analysis.

## Product Purpose

Replace fragile spreadsheet-based health-finance reporting with authenticated, controlled, and auditable workflows. The product should make routine data entry reliable, preserve fiscal traceability, surface timely operational insight, and support consistent reporting across MDAs, programme areas, LGAs, facilities, budgets, and AOP activities.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and accountable. The interface should feel like a durable civic operations tool that helps users complete serious finance work efficiently, without marketing-style ornament or bureaucratic ceremony.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, oversized hero metrics, ceremonial government branding, dense spreadsheet imitation, ambiguous workflow states, and interfaces that sacrifice legibility for visual novelty. The product should not rely on color alone to communicate status or require users to rediscover familiar navigation and form patterns.

## Design Principles

1. Put the active administrative task first and keep navigation predictable.
2. Make every financial record easy to trace, scan, and audit.
3. Prevent inconsistent data through controlled inputs and guidance at the point of action.
4. Prefer calm density and clear hierarchy over decorative surfaces.
5. Keep role and MDA scope visible so users understand the context of every action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, accessible names and titles, sufficient text and status contrast, and redundant non-color status cues. Respect reduced-motion preferences, support long institutional labels without clipping, and keep core reporting and data-entry workflows usable with keyboard and assistive technology.
