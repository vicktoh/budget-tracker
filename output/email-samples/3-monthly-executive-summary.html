FY2026 health budget: 36.9% spent, 16 of 21 MDAs behind on reporting

Official actuals through 30 Jun 2026 (Q1–Q2) · monthly tracking through June · no Q3 entries received yet.

At a glance

With half the year's actuals in, the health sector has spent ₦79.17bn of its ₦214.84bn approved budget (36.9%), about 13 points behind a straight-line pace of 50%. Releases of ₦87.57bn (40.8%) are only slightly ahead of spending, so slow cash release, not slow spending, is the main constraint. KCHIMA is the exception, holding ₦4.25bn that has been released but not spent.

Spending is concentrated: MoH HQ and HMB account for 87% of all expenditure. Reporting is the weakest area. Only 5 MDAs submitted June monthly tracking, no MDA has recorded a Q3 entry, and 19 of 21 MDAs have nobody on the platform to receive reminders.
